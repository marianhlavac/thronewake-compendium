# About the Compendium

An unofficial, community-sourced knowledge base for **[Thronewake](https://thronewake.com)** — a free-to-play, persistent browser strategy game inspired by Travian.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **About this wiki.** Thronewake has no official wiki. Everything here is reconstructed from the game's official Discord: the developer's own posts (developer handle **Nimo**, real name **Wynfir/"Wyn"**), the knowledge-base channels, the changelog, and player Q&A. Statements attributed to the developer are treated as authoritative. Player-supplied details are marked *(player-reported)* or *(unconfirmed)* where they could not be corroborated.
>
> **Version warning.** The game is in **active development** and mechanics change often. The current world, **[The Fourth Awakening](Worlds-and-Server-Speed.md)**, began **29 July 2026**. Information from *before* that date may be outdated; where two sources conflict, this wiki prefers the newer one and notes the difference. See [Worlds & Server Speed](Worlds-and-Server-Speed.md) and the changelog history for context.

## Conventions used here
- **Authoritative** = stated by the developer (Nimo/Wynfir).
- *(player-reported)* = stated by experienced players; generally reliable but not dev-confirmed.
- *(unconfirmed)* = mentioned but never clearly settled in the source.
- **1× values** are the base game numbers; on a speed world, *movement/travel speed, production per hour, and queue/build/train times* scale with the world speed, but **capacities do not** (see [Worlds & Server Speed](Worlds-and-Server-Speed.md)).
