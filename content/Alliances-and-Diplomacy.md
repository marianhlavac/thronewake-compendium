# Alliances & Diplomacy

Alliances are groups of players who share defense, chat, [reports](Reports-and-Map.md), and bonuses, and who coordinate toward the [endgame](Endgame-Ancient-Monument.md). Alliances are managed from the alliance page (the "handshake" icon in the top bar) and joined via the **[Embassy](Buildings.md#embassy)**.

## Alliance basics
- **Member limit:** the max alliance size has been kept **small** — 15 at one point, and **6 without spending resources to expand** on the current world *(player-reported for the Fourth Awakening)*. Small alliances make membership spots valuable and were a deliberate design lever.
- **Bonuses (Alliance Bonuses):** members **donate resources** to unlock shared bonuses — **troop training speed, [Culture Point](Culture-Points.md) production, troop strength, and [merchant](Marketplace-and-Trade.md) capacity**. Bonuses are **persistent** (never reset once unlocked). The troop-strength bonus applies **on top of [Smithy](Buildings.md#smithy)** upgrades. You can see each member's total donated. Food contributions are weighted 0.5 *(player-reported)*.
- **Rights:** roles carry rights such as *Manage diplomacy*, *View alliance reports*, *View member incoming attacks*, *View member activity*, *Manage map markers*, *Manage chats*. With the right rights you can see whether members are under attack and (with "View member activity") a colored last-active indicator.
- **Alliance reports:** you can view alliance attack/scout reports (from members who were attacker or main defender) if you have the right; reports can also be shared manually. A red dot appears on the alliance icon when any member has an incoming attack.
- **Leaving rules:** if you leave an alliance you can't rejoin it for **24h**; after leaving, both parties can't attack each other for **6h**; you can't join an alliance that attacked you (or that you attacked) in the last **24h**. Leaving auto-recalls reinforcements to/from that alliance.
- You **cannot attack or raid members of your own alliance or pact** (and such attacks give **no loot/score**); the **[friendly-attack](Villages-Cities-and-Expansion.md#loyalty--conquering)** toggle is the exception.

## Diplomacy states (between alliances)
- **War** — a formal hostile state. War **blocks trading** between the two alliances (update 24/07). It does **not** gate attacks — you can attack any non-allied, non-pacted player regardless of a declared war.
- **Trade Agreement** — a friendly trade relationship (raises [market](Marketplace-and-Trade.md) send limits between members).
- **Defensive Pact** (renamed from **"Alliance Pact"** on 24/07) — members can reinforce each other and share chat channels; **each member gives up 10% of their resource production per pact** to keep it active (this **stacks** — three pacts = 30%). Creating a Defensive Pact auto-creates a shared General chat channel; a multi-alliance pact chat requires every alliance to have a pact with every other.
- **Treaty** (added 19/07) — you can create shared chat channels and **cannot attack** the other alliance while active. Reports shared with "Alliance" scope are visible to Treaty members (01/08).

**Pact rules:** you can't create a pact if either side attacked the other in the last 24h; once created a pact can't be ended within 24h; after ending, it can't be recreated for 24h; ending a pact starts a 6h no-attack window.

## Personal Pacts (between individuals)
Made in your **[Embassy](Buildings.md#embassy)** with another player:
- Lets you **send reinforcements** to each other, and you **cannot attack** each other.
- Up to **5** personal pacts; **not** with your own alliance members; not with someone if either of you attacked the other in the last 24h. Ending one starts a 6h no-attack window.

## Rewards for allies
When an alliance wins the world (completes the [Ancient Monument](Endgame-Ancient-Monument.md) to 100), **alliances that had an active pact with the winner at that moment also receive rewards** (27/05).
