# Beginner Protection

**Beginner Protection (BP)** shields new players from hostile actions for a period at the start of a [world](Worlds-and-Server-Speed.md), giving time to establish an economy before PvP begins.

## Duration
Duration depends on the world:
- On **10× / early** worlds: **24 hours**, with an optional **+24 hour** extension.
- On **3×** worlds: **3 days**, with an optional **+3 day** extension (up to 6 days total).

The extension is available **once per world**; the extend prompt appears when there are a few hours left (originally ~6h; players later reported seeing it with ~18h remaining).

## Ending Beginner Protection
- BP **ends early if you attack or raid another player** — and, as of update 09/08, it ends when the attack/raid **arrives**, not when it is sent.
- You can still choose to extend BP even after deciding to end it via an attack.

## What you can and can't do during BP
- You **can** attack/raid **[Wilder Sites](Wilder-Sites.md)** during BP (raiding was always possible; attacking is allowed, but it **won't reduce a site's loyalty** during BP, to stop "free" conquests before Ancient troops spawn).
- **Resource sending** is restricted: the free **"gift"** feature and any trade **better than 1:1** are disabled (anti multi-account funneling). **Same-IP** trades are capped at 1:1 / 1000 res / no multi-delivery. **Sending to your own villages is allowed.** (See [Marketplace → send limits](Marketplace-and-Trade.md#send-push-limits).)
- You can update your **[Safe Time](Safe-Time.md)** instantly (no 24h wait) during BP.
- A **guest account** (no email) is valid only during the initial BP period; after that you must log in to continue.
- The map shows an **icon** when a player has BP (or Safe Time) active.
