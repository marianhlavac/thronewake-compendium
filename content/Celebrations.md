# Celebrations & the Festival Grounds

**Celebrations** (players call them "parties") are hosted in the **Festival Grounds** building to generate **[Culture Points](Culture-Points.md)** — the main active way to fuel [expansion](Villages-Cities-and-Expansion.md). The Festival Grounds was added in update 22/05.

## The two celebrations
- **Small celebration — "Tea Party"**: grants the village's **daily CP production, capped**. Caps: **500** on 1× worlds, **250** on 3× worlds.
- **Large celebration — "Grand Ball"**: unlocked at **Festival Grounds level 10**; grants more, capped at **2000** on 1× / **1000** on 3×.

A small party is worth roughly **1 hour of the village's resource production** in CP, which makes celebration-spam a very strong (arguably near-mandatory) CP strategy — though building the Festival Grounds and paying for parties is itself a real resource cost, so it trades off against troops/buildings.

## Durations & cooldowns
- Upgrading the **Festival Grounds reduces the celebration cooldown** (and slightly the duration). It does **NOT** increase CP per party.
- On a **3× world**: a small party ≈ just under **12 hours**, a large party ≈ just under **24 hours** — so roughly one or two smalls and one large per day. (On 1×, a level-10 grand celebration is ~43 hours.)
- You can **queue multiple parties** to run one after another (not simultaneously), so you don't have to be online exactly when one ends (update 24/07).

## Cities & celebrations
A **[City](Villages-Cities-and-Expansion.md#cities)** produces +200 CP/day, which counts toward the CP a Grand Ball can award (up to the cap) if the account is under the cap's daily CP.

## Stormfang: the Stormbrew Celebration
The **[Stormfang Clans](Stormfang-Clans.md#unique-building-stormbrew-works)** can host a **Stormbrew Celebration** in the **Stormbrew Works** to apply that building's attack bonus across **all** their villages at once (rather than needing a separate celebration in each village). Note this also triggers the Stormbrew downsides (weaker [leaders](Units.md#leaders-chiefs), random [catapult](Combat.md#catapults) targeting).
