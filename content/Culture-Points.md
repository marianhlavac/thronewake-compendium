# Culture Points (CP)

**Culture Points** are the resource that lets you **expand** — you need them to found or conquer new [villages](Villages-Cities-and-Expansion.md) and to upgrade a village into a **[City](Villages-Cities-and-Expansion.md#cities)**.

## How Culture Points work
- **Every building** you construct produces CP for the village it is in. You can see a village's CP production rate in its **[Residence or Palace](Villages-Cities-and-Expansion.md#residence--palace)** (or by clicking an empty map tile → "Settle new village").
- CP accumulate **account-wide** into **CP slots**. Each new slot costs **progressively more** CP than the last.
- **Founding or conquering** a new village **consumes one CP slot**. Upgrading a village to a **City** also consumes one CP slot (but not an expansion slot).
- The **first 5 villages** used a lower (the "old") CP requirement; from the 6th onward it became much more expensive (this steepening was once a bug, then reportedly made intentional). **This may since have been fixed** so the requirement no longer steepens this way — *unconfirmed*.
- In your Residence/Palace you can see **how long until your next CP slot unlocks**.

CP figures/tables are based on Travian numbers *(player-reported; some CP details differ)*.

## Getting Culture Points faster
- **[Celebrations](Celebrations.md)** in the **Festival Grounds** are the main active CP source. A **small celebration (Tea Party)** grants the village's daily CP up to a cap; a **large celebration (Grand Ball)** grants more. Caps: on 1× worlds **500 (small) / 2000 (large)**; on 3× worlds **250 / 1000**. A small party gives roughly **1 hour of the village's resource production** worth of value — strong enough that many players treat celebration-spam as near-mandatory for CP.
- **[Embassy](Buildings.md#embassy)** — cheap and high-CP; players level it specifically to expand faster ("CP goat").
- **[Cities](Villages-Cities-and-Expansion.md#cities)** — +200 CP/day and a **25% passive CP bonus** from all buildings/fields.
- **Academies, Herbalists** and other buildings all add CP; some players build "extra" copies just for the CP.
