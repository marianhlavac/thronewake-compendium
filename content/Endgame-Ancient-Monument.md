# Endgame: the Ancient Monument

To **win the world**, your [alliance](Alliances-and-Diplomacy.md) must be the **first to upgrade an Ancient Monument to level 100**. This is Thronewake's endgame, analogous to the Travian World Wonder (players call it the "WW" or "Wonder"). On a **3× world**, the [Construction Plans](#construction-plans) needed to raise it release on **day 60** ([artefacts](Artefacts.md) release earlier, day 30). See [Worlds & Server Speed](Worlds-and-Server-Speed.md#endgame-timing-per-world-speed).

## Getting a Monument village
Ancient Monuments can only be built in special **[Ancient](The-Ancients.md)** villages:
- **Ancient Stronghold villages**
- **Ancient Monument / Ancient Wonder villages**

Your alliance must first **conquer** one of these from the Ancients. Existing Ancient strongholds don't hold artefacts — they only contain the **building slot** for the Monument. After you conquer one, the **Ancients try to take it back** after a short time. Because you **cannot build a [Residence or Palace](Villages-Cities-and-Expansion.md#residence--palace)** there, these villages aren't protected from conquest the normal way — so **raise the Monument to at least level 1 as soon as possible** to protect it.

## Special rules of Monument villages
- You **cannot** turn them into a **[City](Villages-Cities-and-Expansion.md#cities)**.
- You **cannot** build a **[Watch Tower](Buildings.md#watch-tower)** there. *(This "no wall" rule is why endgame defense is so demanding — see the balance note below.)*
- **[Safe Time](Safe-Time.md) does not apply** — anyone can attack them at any time, even during their own Safe Time.
- You **can** build the **[Great Warehouse and Great Granary](Buildings.md#great-buildings)** there, with **3× more** storage capacity.
- Buildings there complete **50% faster**.
- Troops there consume **50% less food** (after the Construction Plan artefacts release).

## Construction Plans
To upgrade a Monument you need a **Construction Plan artefact** (a.k.a. Ancient Monument Plan; see [Artefacts](Artefacts.md)). These come from special **Plan Sanctuary villages** that **spawn later** in the world — an in-game announcement / countdown is made **7 days before** they appear. **Six villages holding a plan artefact spawn** (i.e. there are **6 Construction Plans** on the world) — in the Third Awakening one side claimed 5 of the 6.

Two ways to get a Construction Plan:
1. **Conquer** a Plan Sanctuary village (or a player village that holds the artefact), or
2. Build a **[Treasury](Buildings.md#treasury)** in one of your villages, attack a Plan Sanctuary (or a player village holding the plan), and **destroy its Treasury** (then take the plan with a winning attack from your empty-Treasury village).

### Plan requirements (authoritative)
- For **Monument levels 1–49**, you need **1 Construction Plan** yourself.
- For **levels 50–100**, your alliance needs a **second Construction Plan**.
- The second plan must be held by **another member of your alliance** — **you cannot hold both plans yourself**, and **pacts do not count** for the second plan (it must be an actual alliance member).

The artefact/Treasury holding a plan can be in **any** of your villages — it does **not** have to be in the Monument village.

## Ancient attacks on the Monument
Starting at **level 10**, the Ancients attack your Monument **every 5 levels**, including **siege** units. They also attack (without siege) at level **5** and at **96/97/98/99**. There is **no attack at level 100**.
- They try to **destroy** it, so your alliance must be prepared to defend.
- Because there's no attack at 100, you can **rush the final levels** and complete the Monument to 100 **before a pending siege lands** — but you must have the full resources ready (jumping several levels near the end needs a huge amount, e.g. ~15M resources to bridge lvl 95→100 before that siege).
- Reaching level 100 = **win**; end-of-world [medals](Leaderboards-Medals-and-Store.md) freeze at that moment.

## Resources & storage needed
Building to level 100 needs on the order of **~1,000,000 of each resource** stored, which is why the **[Great Warehouse/Great Granary](Buildings.md#great-buildings)** and coordinated alliance supply are essential. In an **Ancient Wonder/Stronghold village you can build the Great Warehouse/Granary *without* the [Great Storage Plan](Artefacts.md) artefact** (unlike normal villages, which need it) — and there they hold **3× more**. The **[Stone Shield](Artefacts.md)** artefact protects buildings but has **no effect on the Monument itself**; the **[Shadow Veil](Artefacts.md)** doesn't stop enemies targeting the Monument either. See [Artefacts → Ancient/Monument villages](Artefacts.md#artefacts-and-your-ancient--monument-villages-authoritative-nimo).

## Ancient-owned strongholds & difficulty
Ancient Stronghold/Wonder villages near the map **center** tend to have **fewer** Ancient defenders but are **harder to hold**; those near the **edge** have **more** Ancient defense but are easier to defend *(player observations)*.

## Balance note (the "no wall" problem)
Because Monument villages **can't have a Watch Tower**, they have no wall — which strongly encourages players to "sit on" a huge suicide **hammer** all game just to blow the Monument from a high level back down to 0. Community feedback on the endgame (whether the Monument should have a wall, whether the Ancients are too strong, whether required level should be 50 vs 100, etc.) is ongoing and has driven several balance changes.

## The Ancients' own Monument (optional)
On worlds where the toggle is on, the **Ancients also race to build a Monument** to end a stalling world. See [Worlds & Server Speed → safeguard](Worlds-and-Server-Speed.md#world-length--the-infinite-server-safeguard-authoritative). Winning [alliances'](Alliances-and-Diplomacy.md) **pact partners** also receive rewards at the moment the Monument hits 100.
