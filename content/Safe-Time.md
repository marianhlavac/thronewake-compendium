# Safe Time

**Safe Time** is a personal daily protection window of **up to 6 hours per day**. During your Safe Time you are protected from most hostile actions — but you also **cannot launch** most actions against others. It is one of Thronewake's signature departures from Travian (a substitute for the lack of a hero/night-truce), and its exact rules have been iterated on many times.

## Main rule (authoritative)
Attacks, raids, scouts, and reinforcements are **blocked** when **any** of these is true:
- **you** are in Safe Time when sending;
- the **target** is in Safe Time when sending;
- **you** would be in Safe Time when the action **arrives**;
- the **target** would be in Safe Time when the action **arrives**.

This applies to both villages and [Wilder Sites](Wilder-Sites.md).

## Exceptions (actions still allowed during Safe Time)
- Send actions to **your own** villages and your own Wilder Sites.
- **Recall** your reinforcements, or **send back** foreign reinforcements.
- Send **reinforcements** to someone who is in their Safe Time.
- Send reinforcements even when they would **arrive during your own** Safe Time.
- Attack/raid/scout an **Ancient Capital/Stronghold/Wonder** village owned by the **Ancients**.
- Attack/raid/scout an Ancient **Capital/Stronghold/Wonder** village owned by a **player** — but if you do, **your attacking village loses its Safe-Time protection until the next day** (12/07).

> [!NOTE]
> As of update 19/07 you can **no longer** attack **normal** Ancient villages or **unowned** Wilder Sites during your own Safe Time (only the special Ancient Capital/Stronghold/Wonder villages remain attackable, with the protection-loss penalty above).

## Diplomacy still applies
Safe Time does not ignore [diplomacy](Alliances-and-Diplomacy.md). You can only attack valid hostile targets, and only reinforce players you're allowed to support (alliance/pact members).

## Setting & changing Safe Time
- Enable it in **Settings**. It can be **up to 6 hours** per day.
- A change **takes 24 hours to activate**. During that wait you can update it within a 1-hour window without restarting the 24h timer; a change after that starts a fresh 24h wait. A pending change can be updated without resetting the timer.
- You may change your Safe Time at most **3 times per world** (the first activation doesn't count, and changes within 30 minutes of your current time don't count) (12/07).
- Your new Safe Time only takes effect after all currently-incoming attacks that would land in the new window have resolved (you can't retroactively dodge an incoming attack by moving Safe Time onto it).
- During Beginner Protection you can update Safe Time instantly (no 24h wait).

## Related
