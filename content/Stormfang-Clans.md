# Stormfang Clans 🐺

The Stormfang Clans are the low-cost, fast-training, attack-focused tribe. Pathstalkers are trained in the Barracks, making Stormfang the only playable tribe whose scout does not require the Stable.

## Merchants and Watch Tower

- Merchants carry **1,000** resources, the largest base cart, at base speed **12**, the slowest merchant speed.
- The Watch Tower adds **6 flat defense per level**, uses a defense base of **1.02 per level**, and has **5× durability**.
- At Watch Tower level 20, that is 120 flat defense and approximately a 48.6% defense bonus.
- Stormfang has no inherent Shelter-bypass rule.

## Unique building: Stormbrew Works

The Stormbrew Works is capital-only and requires **Rally Point level 10** and **Granary level 20**. It hosts the special [Stormbrew Celebration](Celebrations.md#stormbrew-celebration).

While that celebration is active in the profile's current capital at battle time:

- attacks launched from every village in the profile gain **1% offense per Stormbrew Works level**, up to 20%;
- catapult targets are forced to random hits; and
- leaders reduce loyalty by only **50%** of their normal amount.

An ordinary small or large Festival Grounds celebration does not activate Stormbrew. The effect is tied specifically to an active Stormbrew Celebration and the capital's current Stormbrew Works level.
