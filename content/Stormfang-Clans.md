# Stormfang Clans 🐺

One of the three playable [tribes](Tribes.md). The Stormfang Clans are the **cheap, aggressive, high-volume** tribe — very strong for aggressive play, fast rebuilding, and frequent [raiding](Combat.md).

## Strengths & weaknesses
- Troops are **weaker individually**.
- Troops are **much cheaper and train quickly**.
- Very strong for **aggressive play, rebuilding, and frequent raiding**.
- The **only tribe that can build scouts in the [Barracks](Buildings.md) instead of the [Stable](Buildings.md)**.
- *(Community)* **Strong early game** (cheap, spammable troops dominate early [Wilder Site](Wilder-Sites.md) farming) but tends to be **out-scaled later**. Considered the best **attack-power-per-hour** tribe.

## Merchants & Watch Tower (authoritative)
- **[Merchants](Marketplace-and-Trade.md):** carry **1000**, speed **12** — the biggest per-trip capacity but the slowest merchants.
- **[Watch Tower](Buildings.md#watch-tower):** durability **5×** (needs five times as many rams to destroy — the toughest wall), flat defense **+120**, defense bonus **+48.6% at level 20** (the lowest flat/% defense of the three, offset by extreme durability).

## Unique building: Stormbrew Works
The **Stormbrew Works** can only be built by the Stormfang Clans.
- Gives an **attack bonus while a [celebration](Celebrations.md) is active** (up to **+20%**).
- **Downside:** [leaders](Units.md#leaders-chiefs) become **50% less effective** during the effect.
- **Downside:** your **[catapults](Combat.md#catapults) can only target randomly** while the bonus is active.
- **Update (21/06):** you can host a **Stormbrew Celebration in the Stormbrew Works itself** so the effect applies across **all** your villages, instead of needing a separate [Festival Grounds](Celebrations.md) celebration in each village.

## Units *(reported names — see the caveat on [Units](Units.md))*
Stormfang unit names that appear in the source:
- **Raider** — cheap, fast attacking cavalry; extremely cost-effective for looting (a core early-game farming unit; low cost, ~250 res *(player-reported)*).
- **Axeman** ("axes") — infantry.
- **Blood Charger** — attacking cavalry (high attack).
- **Slave Militia** — an extremely cheap, spammable unit; a weak defender ("best troop in the game" as a joke because you get so many for the resources).
- **Club** ("clubby") — cheap infantry (clubswinger-type).

> [!NOTE]
> No tribe ignores a percentage of a defender's [Shelter](Buildings.md#shelter). The developer explicitly corrected a claim that Stormfang bypasses shelter — in Travian only a *hero* does that, and Thronewake has no hero. See [Tribes → shared correction](Tribes.md#important-shared-correction).
