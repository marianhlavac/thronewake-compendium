# Tribes

Each profile chooses one of three playable tribes when joining a world. The choice fixes that profile's unit roster, unique building, merchant statistics, Watch Tower characteristics, and a small set of economic rules. The [Ancients](The-Ancients.md) are a fourth, non-playable faction.

## Comparison

| Mechanic | [Embermark Dominion](Embermark-Dominion.md) | [Verdant Wardens](Verdant-Wardens.md) | [Stormfang Clans](Stormfang-Clans.md) |
|---|---|---|---|
| General style | Balanced and expensive | Fast and defensive | Cheap and aggressive |
| Unique building | Rider's Wells | Thornsnare Grove | Stormbrew Works |
| Merchant capacity | 500 | 750 | 1,000 |
| Merchant base speed | 16 | 24 | 12 |
| Trade Office scaling | Double the normal per-level gain | Normal | Normal |
| Watch Tower flat defense at level 20 | 200 | 160 | 120 |
| Watch Tower defense multiplier at level 20 | 1.03²⁰, about 1.806 | 1.025²⁰, about 1.639 | 1.02²⁰, about 1.486 |
| Watch Tower durability | 1× | 2× | 5× |
| Shelter capacity | Normal | 1.5× | Normal |

Merchant capacity is per merchant and does not multiply with server speed. Merchant and army travel use the world's [movement-speed bracket](Worlds-and-Server-Speed.md), while many production and capacity systems use raw speed.

## Tribe mechanics

**Embermark Dominion** uses separate active construction lanes for buildings and resource fields. It can therefore run one of each simultaneously. The Second Work Crew research adds a flexible lane, allowing three simultaneous upgrades.

**Verdant Wardens** receive 50% more Shelter capacity. Their Thornsnare Grove captures hostile troops and permanently earns faster outgoing reinforcements as its lifetime capture count grows.

**Stormfang Clans** can train their Pathstalker scout in the Barracks. A capital Stormbrew Works can run a profile-wide Stormbrew Celebration that improves attacks while imposing random catapult targets and weaker leaders.

No playable tribe inherently bypasses Shelter protection.

## Unique-building access

All players can see tribe-specific building entries, but only the matching tribe can construct them. Conquering a village does not grant another tribe's unique construction ability; normal conquest cleanup and tribe requirements determine what remains usable.
