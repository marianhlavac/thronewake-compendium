# Tribes

At the start of a [world](Worlds-and-Server-Speed.md) you choose one of three playable tribes. Each has its own strengths, weaknesses, [units](Units.md), a **unique building**, and different **[merchant](Marketplace-and-Trade.md)** and **[Watch Tower](Buildings.md#watch-tower)** stats. The choice matters, but all three are meant to be viable; the developer describes them as "very recognizable" to Travian players.

The three tribes are:
- [Embermark Dominion](Embermark-Dominion.md) 🔥 — the balanced, expensive, hard-hitting tribe.
- [Verdant Wardens](Verdant-Wardens.md) 🌿 — the fast, mobile, defensive/utility tribe.
- [Stormfang Clans](Stormfang-Clans.md) 🐺 — the cheap, aggressive, high-volume tribe.

A fourth, non-playable faction, **[the Ancients](The-Ancients.md)** 🗿, controls NPC villages and [Wilder Sites](Wilder-Sites.md) and drives the [endgame](Endgame-Ancient-Monument.md).

> [!NOTE]
> The developer changed a tribe design mid-development: the Embermark simultaneous-queue perk moved from being an innate extra queue slot to something tied to a [research](Research.md) keystone (see the tribe page). More tribes may be added in the future.

## At-a-glance comparison

| | [Embermark 🔥](Embermark-Dominion.md) | [Verdant 🌿](Verdant-Wardens.md) | [Stormfang 🐺](Stormfang-Clans.md) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Role** | Balanced offense & defense; strong but expensive army; some of the strongest infantry | Fastest tribe; raids, fast support, map control; strong defensive cavalry | Weaker but cheap, fast-training troops; aggressive raiding & rebuilding |
| **Unique building** | [Rider's Wells](Embermark-Dominion.md#unique-building-riders-wells) | [Thornsnare Grove](Verdant-Wardens.md#unique-building-thornsnare-grove) | [Stormbrew Works](Stormfang-Clans.md#unique-building-stormbrew-works) |
| **Merchant carry** | 500 | 750 | 1000 |
| **Merchant speed** | 16 | 24 | 12 |
| **Trade Office bonus** | Doubled | normal | normal |
| **[Watch Tower](Buildings.md#watch-tower) durability** | 1× | 2× | 5× |
| **Watch Tower flat defense** | +200 | +160 | +120 |
| **Watch Tower def. bonus @ lvl 20** | +80.6% | +63.9% | +48.6% |
| **[Shelter](Buildings.md#shelter)** | normal | protects **+50%** more | normal |

*(Merchant capacity does not scale with world speed; movement speed effectively does — see [Worlds & Server Speed](Worlds-and-Server-Speed.md).)*

### Tribe balance notes *(community consensus)*
- **Stormfang** dominates the **early game** thanks to cheap, fast, spammable troops (great for early [raiding](Combat.md) and [Wilder Site](Wilder-Sites.md) farming) but tends to be out-scaled later.
- **Embermark** fields **stronger individual units** (expensive, hard-hitting) rather than a stronger army overall. The popular "weak early, strong late" characterisation is **disputed by players** and shouldn't be taken as established.
- **Verdant** is the mobility/utility tribe: fastest troops, the best **defensive cavalry** in the game, cheaper [settlers](Villages-Cities-and-Expansion.md), Wilder-Site utility via traps, and stronger Shelters.

## The unique tribe buildings
Each tribe has one building only it can construct. **Tribe-unique buildings are visible to all tribes** in the build menu (so you can see what an opponent could have), but only the owning tribe can build them.

- **[Rider's Wells](Embermark-Dominion.md#unique-building-riders-wells)** (Embermark): reduces food upkeep and training time for Embermark cavalry in that village.
- **[Thornsnare Grove](Verdant-Wardens.md#unique-building-thornsnare-grove)** (Verdant): builds **traps** that capture incoming enemy attackers.
- **[Stormbrew Works](Stormfang-Clans.md#unique-building-stormbrew-works)** (Stormfang): grants an attack bonus while a [celebration](Celebrations.md) is active.

## Important shared correction
No tribe **ignores a percentage of a defender's [Shelter](Buildings.md#shelter)**. In Travian only a *hero* attack bypasses shelter; Thronewake has no hero, so **shelters are never bypassed by a tribe trait**. (A [research](Research.md) exists that helps bypass shelters somewhat, but that is research, not a tribe bonus.)
