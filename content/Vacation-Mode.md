# Vacation Mode

**Vacation Mode** lets you take a break from the game. Activate it in **Settings** for **1 to 15 days** (added 25/06).

## What it does
The developer describes it as **"like the game is paused, but only for you"**:
- **No combat** (you cannot send attacks, raids, scouts, or reinforcements — and you are protected).
- **No trades.**
- **No upgrades of any kind.**
- You **still accumulate resources**, but you **cannot spend** them.
- Entering Vacation Mode **releases your [Wilder Sites](Wilder-Sites.md)** — you can't own Wilder Sites while on vacation (the release becomes irreversible after ~2 minutes).

> [!NOTE]
> An earlier developer statement (when the feature launched) said an **already-queued** building upgrade would **continue** during vacation; a later description said "no upgrades of any kind." Treat the behavior of a pre-existing queue as **unconfirmed**.

## Restrictions
- You **cannot enter Vacation Mode while you hold an [Ancient Monument](Endgame-Ancient-Monument.md)**.
- If an **[artefact](Artefacts.md)** is en route to you and you're in Vacation Mode when it arrives, it **returns to its origin** village (01/07).

Because of these restrictions (and the fact that Wilder Sites and the Monument are central to competitive play), Vacation Mode requires preparation and is of limited use to top players mid-world.
