# Vacation Mode

**Vacation Mode** pauses the most important parts of a profile for a chosen period of **1 to 15 whole days**. An active vacation may be extended, but the total duration measured from its original start can never exceed 15 days.

## What is paused

- Other players cannot send hostile missions to the vacationing profile, and the vacationing player cannot dispatch troop movements.
- Lumber, Stone, and Metal production are paused. **Food production continues**, so stationed troops can still be fed.
- Troop-training queues are checkpointed and paused, including partial progress. They resume after vacation.
- New building or field upgrades, troop training, unit research, Smithy upgrades, healing, trap training, celebrations, trades, offers, exchanges, and trade routes are blocked.
- Building or field upgrades that were already paid for continue. Waiting-for-resources pre-queue items are cancelled; paid queue items are preserved.
- Owned [Wilder Sites](Wilder-Sites.md) are scheduled for release. Vacation-start releases are cancellation-locked.

## Starting requirements

Vacation Mode cannot start while any of these blockers remain:

- outgoing troop movements;
- incoming movements from another player;
- foreign reinforcements stationed in your villages or Wilder Sites;
- owned Wilder Sites;
- a player-owned Ancient Stronghold, Ancient Wonder, or Ancient Capital;
- a held [artefact](Artefacts.md);
- active [Beginner Protection](Beginner-Protection.md);
- troops held in traps, or another player's troops held in your traps;
- pending resource shipments between profiles;
- scheduled profile deletion.

The vacation screen can resolve several blockers for you, including cancelling eligible outgoing movements, returning foreign reinforcements, resolving traps, releasing Wilder Sites, and cancelling profile deletion.

## Ending and recovery boost

- Vacation can be aborted only after it has been active for at least **24 hours**.
- After vacation ends, there is a **12-hour cooldown** before another vacation can start.
- A vacation lasting at least 24 hours grants a recovery boost for the exact vacation duration: Lumber, Stone, and Metal production are doubled, and troop training takes half the normal time. Food production and building speed are not boosted.
- If an older recovery boost still had more time remaining, that longer remaining duration is preserved. An active recovery boost is frozen during vacation rather than consumed.

An artefact already travelling to a player who is on vacation when it arrives returns to its origin.
